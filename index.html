Straight answers

Before you ask

Why is there a term?

The build fee doesn't cover the build. The term does. That's how the price stays this low.

What if I don't send photos?

We still post. Your posts just get stronger with real job photos. Late photos roll into next month.

Can I start small and move up?

Yes. Start on Launch with the $1,000 build. When you're ready for Momentum, you pay the $500 difference, not another $1,500. Your plan moves to $700 a month and a new 12 month term starts.

Can I just buy the website?

Yes. Website only is $1,500 for 5 pages or $2,250 for 8, no monthly plan.

Will this get me leads?

We promise the work, not the phone calls. You get a real site and steady posts every month, and a report showing every piece.

What if I leave early?

Pay out the rest of the term and the site files are yours, or the site comes down. Your domain stays yours either way.

Do you run ads or SEO campaigns?

Not on these plans. When you're ready for that, we do it at urbannicheco.com.
